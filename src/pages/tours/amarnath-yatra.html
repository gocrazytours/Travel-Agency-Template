Day-wise Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival Srinagar — Transfer to Pahalgam

Arrival

Arrive Srinagar Airport

Our driver will meet you on arrival with a welcome placard. Receive a complimentary mineral water bottle. Board your 14-seater Tempo Traveller.

Drive

Transfer to Pahalgam (~95 km / 3–4 hrs)

Scenic drive through the Kashmir Valley — passing saffron fields, apple orchards, and the Lidder river gorge. Enjoy the breathtaking landscape.

Afternoon

Check-in at Pahalgam Hotel

Hotel GM Castle / Blue Peak / Star of Kashmir or Similar. Freshen up and relax. Welcome drink on arrival.

Evening

Pahalgam Leisure & Dinner

Explore the beautiful Pahalgam market, stroll along the Lidder river, or simply enjoy the mountain air. Dinner at hotel.

Night

Overnight — Pahalgam

Day 2

Pahalgam → Chandanwari → Sheshnag (Trek 12 km)

Morning

Early Breakfast & Departure to Chandanwari (~16 km)

After an early breakfast, drive to Chandanwari (base camp for the Amarnath Yatra trek). Complete pony/porter/registration formalities.

Trek

Trek Begins: Chandanwari to Sheshnag (12 km)

The sacred Yatra trek begins! Walk through stunning meadows, glacial streams and mountain passes. Pass the Pissu Top viewpoint (altitude ~3,734 m). Trek takes approx. 6–7 hours.

Evening

Arrive Sheshnag (Alt. 3,590 m)

Stunning glacial lake at Sheshnag — believed to be home of the thousand-headed serpent. Settle into your camp tents.

Camp

Overnight Camping at Sheshnag

Dinner at camp. Rest well — tomorrow is another beautiful but demanding day on the Yatra.

Day 3

Sheshnag → Panchtarni (Trek 14 km)

Morning

Early Breakfast at Camp & Trek Resumes

Start trekking after a warm breakfast. The trail ascends through scenic Mahagunas Pass (highest point of the Yatra, ~4,890 m). Breathtaking panoramic views of the Himalayas.

Trek

Trek: Sheshnag to Panchtarni (14 km)

Cross the Mahagunas Top — the highest and most challenging section. Descend through lush meadows of Poshpathri and Panchtarni. Trek takes approx. 7–8 hours.

Evening

Arrive Panchtarni (Alt. 3,840 m)

Named after the five streams (Panch = five, Tarni = streams) that flow here. A holy and scenic resting ground for pilgrims.

Camp

Overnight Camping at Panchtarni

Dinner at camp. Rest up — tomorrow is the most sacred day of the entire Yatra!

Day 4

Panchtarni → Amarnath Cave Darshan → Return (Trek 12 km)

Morning

Early Start (2–3 AM) — Trek to Amarnath Cave (6 km)

Begin the most spiritually significant leg of the Yatra in the early hours. The anticipation and devotion among pilgrims is electric as you approach the holy cave.

Darshan

Baba Barfani Darshan — Holy Amarnath Cave (Alt. 3,888 m)

A profoundly moving and divine experience — the spiritual highlight of the entire journey. Offer prayers and seek Baba's blessings.

Trek

Return Trek: Cave to Panchtarni (6 km)

After darshan, begin the return trek to Panchtarni. A spiritually fulfilling and physically rewarding walk back through stunning mountain scenery.

Camp

Overnight Camping at Panchtarni

Celebratory dinner at camp. Reflect on today's incredible experience.

Day 5

Panchtarni → Baltal → Sonamarg (Trek 15 km)

Morning

Early Breakfast & Final Trek: Panchtarni to Baltal (15 km)

The last stretch of the Yatra trek — descend from Panchtarni towards Baltal. The path winds through stunning valleys with glacier and river views. Trek takes approx. 6–7 hours.

Afternoon

Arrive Baltal Base Camp

Our driver will be waiting at Baltal. Board the Tempo Traveller for a comfortable transfer to Sonamarg (~15 km, 30 mins).

Evening

Check-in at Sonamarg Hotel

Hotel Barzaman / Neel Gagan or Similar. Freshen up, enjoy the magnificent meadow views, and relax after the successful Yatra.

Night

Dinner & Overnight — Sonamarg

Celebrate the completion of the Amarnath Yatra with a special group dinner!

Day 6

Sonamarg → Srinagar Sightseeing

Morning

Breakfast & Check-out from Sonamarg

Enjoy a leisurely breakfast and check out. Drive to Srinagar (~80 km / 2.5 hrs) through the stunning Sind Valley.

Sightseeing

Srinagar Local Sightseeing

Visit the iconic Mughal Gardens: Shalimar Bagh, Nishat Bagh. Explore the famous Hazratbal Shrine. Browse the vibrant local handicraft markets — pashmina, saffron, walnut carvings, and Kashmiri carpets.

Afternoon

Shikara Ride on Dal Lake (Complimentary — 1 hour)

Enjoy a serene 1-hour complimentary Shikara ride on the legendary Dal Lake. Float past floating gardens, houseboats, and the snow-capped Zabarwan mountains.

Evening

Check-in — Mandalay Houseboat, Dal Lake

Your last night accommodation on a traditional Kashmiri houseboat — a truly unique and memorable experience. Dinner on the houseboat.

Night

Overnight — Mandalay Houseboat, Srinagar

Day 7

Departure — Srinagar Airport

Morning

Breakfast at Houseboat

Enjoy a final Kashmiri breakfast on the beautiful houseboat. Pack up and check out.

Drive

Transfer to Srinagar Airport / Railway Station

Our driver will drop you to the airport or railway station based on your departure time. Final farewells!

Depart

Depart Srinagar

Board your flight / train back home. Thank you for completing the sacred Amarnath Yatra with Go Crazy Tours! Har Har Mahadev! 🙏
