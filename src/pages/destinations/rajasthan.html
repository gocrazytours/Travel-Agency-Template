Destinations Rajasthan Circuit

🏰 Rajasthan Circuit

Forts, Deserts, Lakes & the Colours of the Royal Land

9 Days / 8 Nights Jaipur · Jaisalmer · Jodhpur · Udaipur · Pushkar Starting from Jaipur Airport / Railway Station

Day-wise Itinerary

Day 1

Arrive Jaipur — City of Pink

Arrival

Arrive at Jaipur Airport / Railway Station

Early morning arrival in the Pink City. Our representative will meet you on arrival. Transfer to your hotel, check in and freshen up.

Afternoon

City Palace · Jantar Mantar · Hawa Mahal

Explore the magnificent City Palace, the astronomical wonder of Jantar Mantar (UNESCO World Heritage Site), and the iconic five-storey Hawa Mahal — the Palace of Winds.

Evening

Explore the Local Markets

Stroll through Jaipur's colourful bazaars — jewellery, textiles, handicrafts, and street food await.

Night

Overnight — Jaipur

Day 2

Jaipur Sightseeing — Forts & Palaces

Morning

Amber Fort & Nahargarh Fort

Visit the stunning Amber Fort perched on a hill overlooking Maota Lake — an architectural marvel blending Rajput and Mughal styles. Climb up to Nahargarh Fort for sweeping panoramic views of Jaipur.

Afternoon

Jal Mahal & Albert Hall Museum

Admire the serene Water Palace (Jal Mahal) rising from Man Sagar Lake. Explore the Albert Hall Museum — Rajasthan's oldest museum housed in a grand Indo-Saracenic building.

Evening

Birla Temple & Leisure Time

Visit the beautiful white marble Birla Temple. Enjoy leisure time or opt for local cultural experiences.

Night

Overnight Journey to Jaisalmer

Board the overnight train / bus to the Golden City of Jaisalmer.

Day 3

Jaisalmer — Golden City & Desert Camp

Morning

Arrive Jaisalmer · Check-in & Freshen Up

Morning arrival in the Golden City of Jaisalmer. Check in at your hotel and freshen up after the overnight journey.

Morning

Kuldhara — The Haunted Village

Explore the mysteriously abandoned 13th-century village of Kuldhara — one of Rajasthan's most intriguing ghost towns, deserted overnight by an entire community centuries ago.

Afternoon

Sam Sand Dunes

Head to the Sam Sand Dunes — the heart of the Thar Desert. Enjoy camel rides across the rolling golden dunes.

Evening

Sunset at Sam Sand Dunes · Desert Camp Stay

Witness a breathtaking desert sunset painting the dunes in gold and amber. Spend the night at a desert camp under a canopy of stars — folk music, bonfire, and the magic of the Thar.

Day 4

Jaisalmer — Fort, Havelis & Hidden Gems

Morning

Check-out from Desert Camp · Gadisar Lake

Check out from your desert camp stay. Visit the serene Gadisar Lake — a picturesque reservoir built in the 14th century, dotted with temples and cenotaphs along its ghats.

Afternoon

Jaisalmer Fort & Jain Temples

Explore the golden sandstone Jaisalmer Fort — a living fort where nearly a quarter of the city's population still resides. Admire the exquisitely carved Jain Temples within the fort walls.

Evening

Patwon Ki Haveli & Local Attractions

Visit Patwon Ki Haveli — the most elaborate and grandest of all Jaisalmer's havelis, with five stunning mansions featuring intricate golden-stone carvings. Explore other local attractions at leisure.

Night

Overnight — Jaisalmer

Day 5

Jodhpur — The Blue City

Morning

Early Departure to Jodhpur (5:00 AM)

Early morning departure to Jodhpur. On arrival, check in and freshen up.

Afternoon

Mehrangarh Fort · Jaswant Thada · Toorji Ka Jhalra

Visit the mighty Mehrangarh Fort — one of the largest forts in India, rising 400 feet above the city. Pay respects at the white marble Jaswant Thada cenotaph. Explore the recently restored Toorji Ka Jhalra (stepwell), a stunning slice of Jodhpur's heritage.

Evening

Explore the Blue City — Old Jodhpur

Wander through the iconic blue-washed lanes of Jodhpur's old city, soaking in the colour, culture, and energy of the Sun City.

Night

Overnight — Jodhpur

Day 6

Jodhpur → Pali → Udaipur

Morning

Umaid Bhawan & Mandore Garden

Visit the grand Umaid Bhawan Palace — one of the world's largest private residences, part palace, part heritage hotel, part museum. Explore the tranquil Mandore Garden with its cenotaphs of Jodhpur's rulers.

Afternoon

Kumbhalgarh Fort

Visit the formidable Kumbhalgarh Fort — home to the world's second-longest wall (after the Great Wall of China), stretching 36 km across the Aravalli Hills.

Afternoon

Reach Pali — Om Bana Mandir & Adinath Temple

Stop at Pali to visit the unique Om Bana Mandir (Bullet Baba temple) — a one-of-a-kind roadside shrine famous across Rajasthan. Also visit the Adinath Temple.

Evening

Arrive Udaipur — City of Lakes

Evening arrival in the romantic City of Lakes. Check in, freshen up, and end the day with the gentle atmosphere of Udaipur.

Day 7

Udaipur Sightseeing — Palaces & Temples

Morning

City Palace · Jagdish Temple · Saheliyon Ki Bari

Explore the spectacular City Palace — a sprawling complex of palaces, courtyards, and museums overlooking Lake Pichola. Visit the beautifully carved Jagdish Temple and the serene Garden of Maidens (Saheliyon Ki Bari) with its lotus pools and marble elephants.

Afternoon

Bagore Ki Haveli · Shri Ekling Ji Temple

Visit Bagore Ki Haveli — an 18th-century mansion on the waterfront of Lake Pichola, famous for its evening cultural shows. Drive to the Shri Ekling Ji Temple, a revered 8th-century temple dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Evening

Lakeside Evening — Pichola

Spend a leisurely evening by the shimmering Lake Pichola, watching the palaces and ghats glow in the fading light.

Night

Overnight — Udaipur

Day 8

Udaipur — Lakes, Palaces & Sunsets

Morning

Fatehsagar Lake & Jagmandir Palace

Enjoy a morning at the tranquil Fatehsagar Lake — a large artificial lake flanked by hills and gardens. Take a boat to the island palace of Jagmandir, a jewel rising from the waters of Lake Pichola.

Afternoon

Sajjangarh Monsoon Palace & Ambrai Ghat

Drive up to Sajjangarh — the Monsoon Palace perched atop a hill, offering sweeping views of Udaipur and the Aravalli ranges. Stroll along the atmospheric Ambrai Ghat, one of the most picturesque spots in Udaipur.

Evening

Local Markets & Pichola Lakeside

Browse Udaipur's vibrant local markets for Rajasthani handicrafts, miniature paintings, and silver jewellery. End the evening by the gently lit Lake Pichola.

Night

Overnight — Udaipur

Day 9

Udaipur → Pushkar → Jaipur — Leave with Memories

Morning

Depart Udaipur · Sambhar Salt Lake & Kishangarh Fort

Morning departure from Udaipur towards Pushkar. En route, visit the vast Sambhar Salt Lake — India's largest inland salt lake. Stop at the grand Kishangarh Fort, overlooking the serene Gundalao Lake.

Afternoon

Pushkar Lake & Brahma Temple

Arrive in the holy town of Pushkar. Visit the sacred Pushkar Lake with its 52 bathing ghats. Pay your respects at the famous Brahma Temple — one of the very few temples in the world dedicated to Lord Brahma.

Evening

Pushkar Streets & Evening Aarti

Explore the vibrant, colour-drenched streets of Pushkar — filled with quirky cafes, handicraft stalls, and an unmistakable spiritual energy. Attend the moving evening Aarti ceremony at Pushkar Lake — a truly unforgettable sight.

Depart

Late Evening — Journey to Jaipur & Departure

Late evening transfer to Jaipur Airport / Railway Station. Carry with you the forts, deserts, colours, and countless memories of the Royal Land. Until we meet again! 🙏

Shopping & Food Tips

  • Jaipur — Johri Bazaar, Neehru Bazaar, Tripolia Bazaar & Bapu Bazaar

  • Jaisalmer — Fort Market (camel leather goods, embroidery, fossils)
  • Jodhpur — Sardar Market near the Clock Tower

  • Jaisalmer — Daulat Ke Daal Pakwaan 🫕, The Blue Heaven rooftop cafe ☕
